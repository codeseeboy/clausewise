# Hot Work Permit Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

[HWP-1.1] This policy sets the controls for hot work: welding, cutting, grinding, brazing and any other work that produces sparks or an open flame.

[HWP-1.2] The policy applies to employees and contractors working anywhere on the plant site.

## 2. When a permit is required

[HWP-2.1] A hot work permit is required for any welding, cutting or grinding carried out outside the designated maintenance workshop.

[HWP-2.2] Hot work inside the designated maintenance workshop does not require a permit, provided the workshop fire extinguishers have been inspected within the last 30 days.

## 3. Hot work zones

[HWP-3.1] Hot work is permitted in Zone B (utilities area) with a valid permit and a dedicated fire watch.

[HWP-3.2] Hot work is not permitted in Zone A (tank farm) under any circumstances, including during a plant shutdown.

[HWP-3.3] Hot work in Zone C (loading bay) is permitted only during a planned shutdown and must be approved in writing by the Plant Manager.

## 4. Controls during the work

[HWP-4.1] A fire watch must remain at the hot work site for at least 60 minutes after the work has finished.

[HWP-4.2] The atmosphere must be gas tested before work starts. Hot work must not start if the flammable gas reading exceeds 10% of the lower explosive limit (LEL).

[HWP-4.3] Gas readings must be repeated every 2 hours while hot work continues.

## 5. Permit validity

[HWP-5.1] A hot work permit is valid for one shift, up to a maximum of 12 hours. It cannot be extended; a new permit must be issued for further work.
