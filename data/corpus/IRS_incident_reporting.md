# Incident Reporting Procedure

## 1. Purpose

[IRS-1.1] This procedure explains how incidents and near misses are reported and investigated.

## 2. Reporting

[IRS-2.1] All incidents, including near misses, must be reported to the Shift Supervisor immediately.

[IRS-2.2] A written incident report must be entered in the incident system within 24 hours of the incident.

[IRS-2.3] An incident that causes a lost time injury must be reported to the Plant Manager within 2 hours.

## 3. Investigation

[IRS-3.1] A root cause investigation must be completed within 7 days for every high severity incident.

[IRS-3.2] Near misses do not need a root cause investigation, unless the same near miss happens three times within 30 days.

## 4. Regulator notification

[IRS-4.1] Reportable incidents must be notified to the regulator within 72 hours.

[IRS-4.2] An environmental spill larger than 200 litres must be notified to the regulator within 24 hours.

## 5. Protection for reporters

[IRS-5.1] Employees who report an incident in good faith will not face disciplinary action.
