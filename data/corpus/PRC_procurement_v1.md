# Procurement Policy

## 1. Purpose

[PRC-1.1] This policy sets the rules for buying goods and services for the plant.

## 2. Vendors

[PRC-2.1] A purchase order may only be issued to a vendor registered in the approved vendor list.

[PRC-2.2] New vendors must be approved by the Procurement Head before their first order.

## 3. Quotations

[PRC-3.1] Purchases above INR 200,000 require at least three written quotations from different vendors.

[PRC-3.2] Purchases up to INR 200,000 require one written quotation.

## 4. Single-source purchases

[PRC-4.1] A purchase from a single vendor without competing quotations requires a written justification approved by the Procurement Head.
