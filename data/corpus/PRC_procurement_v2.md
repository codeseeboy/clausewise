# Procurement Policy Amendment

## 1. Changes to quotations

[PRCA-1.1] With effect from 1 March 2026, purchases above INR 200,000 require at least two written quotations instead of three.

[PRCA-1.2] Emergency purchases up to INR 100,000 may be made without a quotation. The Plant Manager must approve the purchase within 24 hours.
