# Access Control Policy

## 1. Purpose

[ACP-1.1] This policy controls who may enter which areas of the plant and how access is granted and removed.

## 2. ID badges

[ACP-2.1] All employees must wear their photo ID badge where it can be seen at all times on site.

[ACP-2.2] Badges must not be shared with or lent to any other person.

## 3. Visitors and contractors

[ACP-3.1] Visitors must be escorted by an employee at all times in the process area.

[ACP-3.2] Visitors are not allowed to enter the control room unless the Shift Supervisor has approved the visit in writing.

[ACP-3.3] Contractors who have completed the site induction may move without an escort in Zone B (utilities area), but not in Zone A (tank farm).

## 4. Access rights

[ACP-4.1] Access to the server room is limited to IT staff named on the server room access register.

[ACP-4.2] Access rights must be reviewed every 90 days by the Department Head.

[ACP-4.3] Access must be removed within 24 hours of an employee leaving the company.

## 5. Security rules

[ACP-5.1] Tailgating, following another person through a controlled door without using your own badge, is prohibited.

[ACP-5.2] A lost badge must be reported to Security within 2 hours.
